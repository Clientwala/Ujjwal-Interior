About Us

Dhanbad's Most Trusted Interior Design Studio

Founded in 2014, Ujjwal Interior has spent over a decade transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary living and working environments across Dhanbad and Jharkhand. From the heart of Saraidhela, we have built a legacy of trust, quality, and creative excellence.

Led by Mr. Ujjwal — a seasoned architect and interior designer with an eye for detail and a passion for perfection — our studio brings together architectural precision and artistic sensibility. Every project we undertake is a commitment: to listen, to design thoughtfully, and to deliver spaces that exceed expectations.

Whether it is a cozy bedroom, a grand residence, a corporate office, or a showroom window display — we approach every canvas with the same dedication. Your space tells your story. We make sure it tells it beautifully.
